# YouTube channels — free-plugin coverage

## Read this first (it changes how you use the list)

**YouTube hides business emails behind a CAPTCHA by design.** On any channel: **About → View email address → solve CAPTCHA**. No tool can collect these, and any list that hands you fifteen ready-made addresses either scraped them years ago (dead) or invented them. So this list gives you the **channel and the route**, and you spend ~30 seconds per channel getting the address yourself.

Contact priority per channel: **business email from About** → **Discord / community links in the description** → **the channel's own submission form if it has one** → **a public reply to a roundup video** (last resort, works surprisingly often on smaller channels).

**Verified live this run:** Bedroom Producers Blog's free-plugin hub, and the existence of active 2026 free-VST roundup videos including *Mack Beats Studio* ("55 FREE VST Plugins You NEED in 2026", "15 FREE VST Plugins every music producer should download in 2026", both within the last month as of writing). The rest of the list is drawn from the well-known free-plugin coverage space — **treat every entry as "verify the channel is alive before writing"**, which is one click.

**Sizes are orders of magnitude, not precise counts.** Check subscriber numbers yourself if it matters to you.

## The email

Keep it under 120 words. They get a lot of these; the ones that get read are the ones that are already finished — no attachments to open, no "hop on a call", nothing to reply to unless they want to.

> **Subject:** Free open-source synth where you build sounds from blocks (VST3/AU, Mac + Win)
>
> Hi — I made a free synthesizer called BLOCKWAVE and thought it might fit a free-plugin roundup.
>
> The idea: every sound is a square wave, and instead of turning knobs you place material blocks on a 3×3 grid to build a patch. Same blocks always make the same sound. Sixteen exact block patterns are secret recipes that unlock signature sounds — three are published, the rest are for people to find.
>
> 128 presets, VST3 + AU + standalone on macOS, VST3 on Windows. GPLv3, no account, no upsell.
>
> Download and press kit: https://nevercsof.github.io/blockwave
> Source: https://github.com/nevercsof/blockwave
>
> No pressure either way — thanks for reading.
>
> — Kirill

**Personalise one line per channel** before sending. A single specific sentence ("your April free-plugin video is what got me to try Vital") beats the whole rest of the email, and mass-identical mail is obvious and gets deleted.

## How to actually work through this

Do **three a day**, not fifteen at once. Log who you contacted and when in a scratch file. Do not follow up more than once, and not sooner than two weeks. If a channel covers it, thank them and leave it there — do not ask for anything else.
